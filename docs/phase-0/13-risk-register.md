# M. Risk Register

L = likelihood, I = impact (1 low – 5 severe), Score = L×I. Owner = who acts. Review at every phase end.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Score | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | **Scope overload** — 35 modules for a solo dev at 5–10 h/week; never reaching a usable product | 5 | 5 | 25 | Release plan R1–R5 with **R1 as the smallest usable pilot** (doc 15); re-baseline checkpoints after Phases 2–3; trim list & cut order; "never cut" list protects security/finance/backups; phase gates | You/me |
| R02 | **Cross-tenant data leak** (child data exposed to another school) | 3 | 5 | 15 | 7 locks, isolation harness in CI, route manifest, lint rules, code review of every repo/aggregation | Me |
| R03 | **Financial errors** (double charge, lost payment, wrong dues) destroy school trust | 3 | 5 | 15 | Immutable ledger, idempotency, transactions, invariants tests, reconciliation job, day-close, pilot parallel-run with Excel | Me |
| R04 | **Unreliable backups / data loss.** Free tiers may lack restorable backups; ₹0 budget tempts skipping them | 4 | 5 | 20 (target residual 15 after gate) | **No real data until Gate G2★ passes:** automated DB+file backups, encrypted, **off-site on a separate provider**, **restore drill passed** with measured RTO/RPO; drill repeated quarterly and refreshed each release; free-tier DB alone is not accepted for production; if budget can't provide this → "not yet" (D-26) | You |
| R05 | **Legal/regulatory non-compliance** (DPDP Act, children's data, payments, GST on receipts) | 3 | 5 | 15 | Privacy-by-design, consent records, retention policy, DPA with schools, **qualified legal review before pilot**; no compliance claims | You |
| R06 | **Collecting school fees via vendor gateway account** may make vendor a regulated intermediary (payment aggregator rules) | 3 | 5 | 15 | MVP records manual payments only; online payments later through each **school's own gateway account** (or provider's split/marketplace product) after legal advice | You |
| R07 | **Free hosting terms prohibit commercial use / sleep / throttle / reclaim; no SLA** — and ₹0 budget pushes toward exactly these | 5 | 4 | 20 | Free tiers used for **dev/staging with synthetic data only**; production host must have **verified commercial-SaaS rights** recorded in `verification-log.md` (Gate G1★); portability rules (doc 12 §11) so migration is a config change; written no-SLA statement to the school; no promise of uptime | You |
| R08 | **Cookie/CORS failure** across different domains breaks login | 4 | 3 | 12 | Same-site deployment (rewrites or one registrable domain) decided up front; E2E login test on staging | Me |

| R10 | **Supply-chain / vulnerable dependencies** | 3 | 4 | 12 | Minimal deps, lockfile, audit in CI, Dependabot, avoid abandoned packages | Me |

| R13 | **Mongo transactions unavailable/slow** on chosen tier or local env | 2 | 4 | 8 | Replica set in tests; verify tier; design critical flows to be idempotent so retry is safe | Me |

| R16 | **Support/staff misuse of access** | 2 | 4 | 8 | Break-glass, no default PII access, audit visible to school | You |
